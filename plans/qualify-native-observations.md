## Risks / unknowns

- **Coordinates and asynchronous events:** parent-local callbacks and separately timed observations may not share a qualified frame/checkpoint. Start with existing window geometry; review any later conversion/coherence claim.
- **Text and actionability limits:** line events may not expose rendered truncation, and driver visibility may not prove an unobscured actionable target. Keep counterexamples and unsupported outcomes rather than adding a guessed provider.
- **Precision and tolerance:** physical rounding may prevent the illustrative fractional distinction. Establish representability before claiming native precision; freeze justified tolerance before measurement.
- **Transport:** earlier target loss remains unexplained. New work needs its own bounded authority and preserves ingress before qualification; this plan does not promise a transport fix or unlimited retries.
- **Scope growth:** broaden a specimen only after the previous unit composes; no generic collector is justified merely by planning future subjects. A reserved contract/provider change needs explicit review.
