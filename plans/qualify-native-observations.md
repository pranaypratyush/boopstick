### Increment 1 — two subjects and relationship detection

PR6 implements this seam; it is not merged or fully qualified by adopting this plan. Review and integrate an accepted increment before expanding it.

1. Reuse actual root/body/action nodes, explicit bindings and the existing window/dp `measureInWindow` handoff. Retain floats, generation/ref/environment guards, durable ingress and external comparison. A declared root reference supports containment. No new `onTextLayout` provider or recursive parent-coordinate converter is needed for this increment.
2. Freeze one independently justified request, allowed variation, gap/alignment/containment/minimum-size predicates, identities and tolerances before collection. Prefer relationships when reflow is allowed; retain independently required absolute placement. Pairwise observations need the same qualified declared/observed space. Missing siblings are unavailable facts, never zero rectangles.
3. Acquire three controls, narrow permitted wrapping, a wrong-gap implementation and restoration through actual native interactions at distinct checkpoints. Keep the request unchanged and retain raw bytes, whole-screen compositor originals, source/build/state identities, failures and incomplete attempts. No direct callback invocation, borrowed earlier-state facts or post-failure baseline retuning.
4. Exercise missing/duplicate/stale/wrong-checkpoint identities, wrong-kind facts and invalid conversion refusal at the affected boundary. Label transformed offline inputs as counterfactuals, not new acquisitions. Preserve unrelated eligible outcomes only where the existing contract permits; global admission/coherence blockers remain global.
5. Replay the same saved numerical findings twice without a device or LLM and bind equal canonical results to identical request/capture bytes. Account for residual custom code, publish a neutral reproduction recipe and verify owned teardown. Three control samples are not a performance/statistical ranking.

### Increment 2 — repeated subjects, text/media and behavior

Only after Increment1 acceptance, extend the same specimen for missing facts. Explicitly bind root/body/media/action and repeated instances; two same-label nodes with distinct instance keys must not be selected by first-label or longest-text heuristics. Use public `onLayout`/`onTextLayout` only where needed, preserving parent-relative raw units/identity and qualifying any common-space conversion. Keep text-line observations and window geometry distinct; do not manufacture mixed-provider relationships.

Qualify intentional media absence through actual unmount and appropriate complete enumeration; retain narrow wrapping/text observations and an actual disclosure expand/collapse cycle. Seed square media against a frozen ratio obligation and a one-line-body defect, then restore without changing expectations. Preserve measured mismatch or explicit unsupported/insufficient evidence, including screenshot counterexamples when numeric rules miss rendered truncation. Line count is not a visible-line/text-fit/ellipsis certificate; unsupported cannot satisfy a UI obligation.

Keep observation out of production entrypoints and preserve ordinary styling/render/interaction with observation disabled. Do not copy component JSX, use private Fabric/Fiber APIs or change flattening globally. Retain the app/story, existing driver and authorized compositor capture. Agent-device remains optional, Harness is not a default structural collector, and neither is an automatically qualified integration or prerequisite. The motivating external evaluations are context, not Boopstick compatibility proof.

### Increment 3 — remaining native qualification limits

After the relevant earlier seam is accepted, qualify the following in the same neutral specimen under a separately reviewed finite scope. Keep each obligation visible even if the provider cannot satisfy it.

| Discriminator | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Readiness success/exhaustion | Establish relevant fonts/images/layout/transitions/keyboard/animation readiness or fail/withhold within the approved bound; an unchanged last sample or network idleness cannot override missing readiness. |
| Fractional undersizing | Establish native representability first: measured43.5 against independent minimum44 with tolerance0 must fail in one declared unit. If unrepresentable, retain that limitation plus separately labelled synthetic arithmetic; no native precision claim follows. |
| Displaced composition | A justified placement predicate fails when the composition moves; recentering/normalization must not erase displacement. |
| Logical scope outsider | Physical overlap or aliases cannot admit an outsider as a requested logical member or prove absence/coverage. |
| Covered action | Geometry may pass while a separately exercised native behavior/actionability obligation fails or remains unsupported; geometry alone cannot approve the whole UI task. |

Preserve original coordinates/floats, origins and applicable density/font scale. Readiness, scope, hit-region and occlusion claims need adequate provider evidence. Layout/compositor observations are not an atomic frame; accessibility bounds, Canvas redraws and screenshots cannot silently substitute for geometry. Retain actual source/build/bundle evidence: manually fetching the same bundle URL does not attest bytes executed by the runtime.
