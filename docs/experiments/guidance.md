# Upstream guidance-method experiments

**Disposition:** evidence-only reconciliation; no justified guidance rewrite. These experiments studied an **upstream UI-verification method**, not qualification of Boopstick's packaged standalone skill and not acceptance of a project UI.

The record comprises six simulated decisions in Wave 1, three simulated reports in Wave 2, a historical twelve-scenario/sixteen-decision crosswalk, and separate semantic and standards reviews against eight historical acceptance criteria. No images were inspected or application, browser, native, producer or comparator campaign executed in these waves. The experiment parent remained planner/orchestrator, not project UI acceptor.

**Evidence access:** all primary reports, prompts, prewritten oracles, source identities, crosswalk and review addenda remain private. Nineteen original artifacts plus nine supplemental artifacts were retained privately; neither artifacts nor manifests are published here. These newly authored summaries identify the evidence without inventing public links or converting private-source identities into public provenance claims. The package's own [skill](../../skills/boopstick/SKILL.md) is a separate subject, not a substitute for those upstream records.

## GUIDE-01 — Preserve historical evidence without relabelling it

- **Question / why:** Which earlier instruction decisions still support the changed method, and which seams require current evidence?
- **Setup:** Inspect all twelve historical scenarios, sixteen actual decisions and eight original acceptance criteria; compare old and changed guidance with explicit compatibility reasoning.
- **Observed result:** The crosswalk accounted for every decision, including stale/missing approval, invalid capture, unsupported continuity, inaccessible/effect-free controls and unavailable native setup. Compatible narrow semantics remained reusable. Changed routing, image-oracle interpretation, normalization and standalone composition needed affected evidence or a source-grounded disposition; the later waves addressed those seams.
- **Failure / correction:** Historical success and candidate byte parity could not establish that changed instructions had freshly executed or that candidate-specific checks remained valid. Original method/run identities were preserved instead of rewritten.
- **Lesson / decision:** Reuse unaffected proof with an impact argument. A changed seam is an evidence need, not automatically a documentation defect or demand to rerun every old scenario.
- **Limits / evidence:** Sixteen decisions are not sixteen independent consumers; the historical contexts are not new-method runs. Private historical crosswalk and independent semantic closure review. No historical campaign was rerun for this reconciliation.

## GUIDE-02 — Wave 1: separate claims, acquisition and continuity

- **Question / why:** Can a fresh consumer distinguish valid narrow results from unsafe completion claims using the changed method?
- **Setup:** Six one-shot simulated cases, supplied relevant references, frozen expected dispositions and no access to the scoring oracle: changed approved design; mismatched acquisition; endpoint-only continuity; maker-replaced baseline; justified non-exact comparison; private standalone evidence.
- **Observed result:** Primary dispositions matched the prewritten oracle. Regression equality did not clear current-design parity failure. Wrong observed viewport/DPR/font state did not satisfy the requested scenario. Endpoint images left same-mounted draft/selection continuity unverified, without inventing actual loss. A maker's replacement baseline could not authorize a difference. Justified tolerance/masking was conditionally usable; private custody could support authorized review.
- **Failure / correction:** Some exact taxonomy/mask/standalone wording was absent from the consulted local guides. The consumer marked derivations and limits rather than treating those omissions alone as proof of a defect. Supplied paths meant this wave tested interpretation, not unassisted route discovery.
- **Lesson / decision:** Parity, regression, measured layout/text and behavior/composition are separate obligations. Observed acquisition conditions matter; declarations and endpoint appearance cannot discharge missing evidence. Preserve valid narrower results while withholding overall completion.
- **Limits / evidence:** Stipulated observations only; no actual capture, interaction, continuity test or accuracy estimate. Private Wave 1 assignment, oracle, raw answers, consumer assessment and independent source review.

## GUIDE-03 — Wave 2: legitimate normalization versus hidden defects

- **Question / why:** Can the method permit justified image comparison without letting transformed green conceal an explicit requirement failure?
- **Setup:** Three standalone simulated reports with approved 16-logical-pixel gap and visible Save label, matched-condition premises, retained raw images and private reviewer access. Declared uniform DPR normalization and a justified 0.25-logical-pixel tolerance were contrasted with local warping and material masking.
- **Observed result:** (1) Reference 16 physical pixels / DPR 1 and candidate 32 / DPR 2 both equal 16 logical pixels; an approved timestamp-only mask outside required subjects supported conditional scoped conformity. (2) Raw candidate 24 / DPR 2 equals 12 logical pixels; locally expanding it to 32 did not repair the raw four-pixel gap defect. (3) Correct spacing survived, but stipulated invisible Save remained a visible-label failure despite an unauthorized label-region mask and green comparison.
- **Failure / correction:** Uniform coordinate conversion preserves logical design; local expansion changes the feature under test. A mask cannot remove a required label from the acceptance obligation. Keep raw and transformed results, diagnose the affected failure, and preserve independently supported unaffected claims.
- **Lesson / decision:** Judge transformations against the obligation and declared mapping, not a blanket exact-pixel rule or tool color. A valid positive control matters as much as refusing defect-hiding green.
- **Limits / evidence:** These arithmetic and semantic decisions used supplied facts, not inspected images or executed image algorithms. No rendered correctness, tolerance portability or comparison-engine qualification follows. Private Wave 2 prompt/oracle/answers and semantic closure review.

## GUIDE-04 — Entrypoint discovery and private standalone review

- **Question / why:** Can a consumer reach the composed upstream method and produce a reviewable private evidence route without being handed UI-guide paths or adopting orchestration machinery?
- **Setup:** Wave 2 began from the upstream method's sole entrypoint; Wave 1 separately exercised a standalone private-evidence scenario. Existing project notes/storage and authorized reviewer access were stipulated.
- **Observed result:** The Wave 2 trace reached role guidance, testing/review, assertion authoring and tools/evidence. It continued into linked comparator documentation despite no supplied wire report. Both waves described source-linked scenario/results and private raw-artifact access followed by authorized review, without requiring public screenshot upload or a new runner, registry or orchestration installation.
- **Failure / correction:** Comparator-document over-reading was an efficiency limitation, not a demonstrated failed route or forced comparator adoption. Later source hashing identified the hashed bytes, not an atomic record of every earlier read. Private artifact access enabled review; it did not constitute review completion or approval.
- **Lesson / decision:** Retain the successful route observation without inventing a routing framework. Bind obligations, candidate/reference/method/scenario identities, actual conditions, results, limits and authorized raw access in existing records.
- **Limits / evidence:** One successful trace is not universal discovery or minimum-context proof. This is standalone use of the upstream method, not Boopstick package-skill qualification. Actual artifact correctness/access was stipulated in the simulations. Private Wave 2 retrieval trace and both independent closure reviews.

## GUIDE-05 — Authority attribution was prompt-confounded

- **Question / why:** Did the consumer's incorrect assignment of project acceptance authority establish a guidance defect?
- **Setup:** Wave 2 repeatedly assigned acceptance/requirement-change decisions to the experiment parent. An independent semantic review initially flagged that attribution; the parent then disclosed launch wording that also called the parent an acceptance owner, despite the narrower user grant.
- **Observed result:** The authority addendum corrected causal attribution: the response was prompt-confounded. Project UI acceptance remained with the actually authorized project owner; the parent could assess this instruction experiment, not accept its hypothetical UIs. No project acceptance occurred.
- **Failure / correction:** Preserve the raw answer, original review and addendum. Do not describe the attribution as an established unprompted model failure or skill defect. Dispatch wording cannot expand user-granted authority, and simulated visual decisions do not depend on making the parent an acceptor.
- **Lesson / decision:** Distinguish experiment-evidence assessment from project requirement/reference/acceptance authority. Inspect launch instructions before diagnosing a source or model defect.
- **Limits / evidence:** Correct owner identification under an uncontaminated prompt was not established. No coached retry or guidance rewrite was justified. Private semantic review, authority addendum and final reconciliation.

## GUIDE-06 — Independent review and evidence custody

- **Question / why:** Do the current decisions, retained history and required receipts justify a source rewrite or a bounded evidence-only disposition?
- **Setup:** Separate source-first semantic and standards/custody reviews, with historical compatibility and candidate-bound receipt assessment kept distinct. The semantic review did not rely on the standards lane's conclusion, or vice versa.
- **Observed result:** Semantic review found no blocking guidance omission; necessary safe derivations plus actual affected probes supported evidence-only reconciliation. Standards review supported reuse of input-bound package/discovery/review receipts, but initially held whitespace coverage and durable custody. Nineteen original artifacts were privately retained and checked for size/hash/byte equality with zero mismatches; a separate nine-artifact supplement retained closure reports, addenda and exact-delta evidence without overwriting the original batch.
- **Failure / correction:** Temporary retrieval alone was not durable custody; source-byte closure was not semantic acceptance. Optional taxonomy, standalone and mask clarifications were not established unmet requirements. The reconciliation preserved those distinctions and resolved the narrow custody/receipt needs instead of rewriting guidance to seek green.
- **Lesson / decision:** Keep semantic evidence, static receipt freshness and retention separate. Preserve raw answers and corrections; a sanitized public lesson is not a replacement for authorized private primary-evidence access.
- **Limits / evidence:** Twenty-eight retained artifacts do not guarantee filesystem lifetime or authorize evidence retirement. These independent reviews support bounded evidence disposition, not full UI-method stage acceptance. Private manifests, closure reviews, custody verification and reconciliation; no raw artifacts or manifests published here.

## GUIDE-07 — Exact-delta checks answer coverage, not behavior

- **Question / why:** Could matching final bytes and patch hunks prove that an older plain whitespace check covered the changed guides?
- **Setup:** Inspect retained check/patch receipts and compare the relevant changed lines, keeping index-to-working-tree coverage distinct from base-to-candidate equality.
- **Observed result:** Matching additions and final bytes did not establish the historical index contents. Already-staged additions could be absent from a plain diff's checked delta. A later explicitly authorized, two-guide commit-to-commit whitespace check ran once and exited zero with empty output/error streams.
- **Failure / correction:** An initially overbroad reuse conclusion was narrowed and retained as history. The explicit delta receipt closed this candidate's whitespace obligation independently of the current index; it did not retroactively prove the old check's coverage.
- **Lesson / decision:** A receipt proves the inputs and comparison it actually observed. Close a precise static evidence gap with an exact scoped check, not an unrelated campaign or a claim that equal end states prove equal check coverage.
- **Limits / evidence:** No behavioral, whole-repository or UI correctness inference. The check belongs to the retained historical increment; authoring this knowledge base did not rerun it. Private whitespace follow-up/coverage challenge, exact-delta receipt and reconciliation.

## GUIDE-08 — Stopping point: evidence-only, not UI acceptance

- **Question / why:** What conclusion does the combined experiment actually authorize?
- **Setup:** Reconcile nine primary simulated decisions, the complete historical crosswalk, successful entrypoint retrieval, separate reviews and scoped receipt/custody corrections.
- **Observed result:** No justified guidance rewrite or missing decisive semantic experiment was established. The bounded increment ended with evidence-only disposition and the authority confound preserved, rather than more diagnostic waves.
- **Failure / correction:** Package availability, comparator success, historical green decisions, static checks and model interpretations cannot substitute for actual project review, live UI evidence or independent real-consumer qualification.
- **Lesson / decision:** Stop when the scoped uncertainty is resolved. Reuse this record for its stated decisions, not as permission to promote a method, change a baseline, accept a UI or launch another campaign.
- **Limits / evidence:** No universal reliability, full UV1 acceptance, UV2 project-method adoption, UV3 independent real-consumer qualification or UV4 original two-consumer obligation is established by this increment. Boopstick publication alone cannot satisfy those separate upstream obligations. Private final reconciliation and independent review addendum; no campaign reruns.
